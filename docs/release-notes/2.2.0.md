**BentoBar 2.2**: Remove deletes an item, a trash to drop it on, a bar that starts in Left, and two items fewer.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Optional online items.** Only **Weather** and **Air quality & UV** (Open-Meteo, no key), **Exchange rates** (Frankfurter, no key), **Flight** (AirLabs, with a free key of your own) and **US stocks** (Finnhub, with a free key of your own) go online, and only after you set them up; **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/Meridian11Labs/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **Shelf and Value from a URL are gone, for now.** BentoBar now asks only the services it names:
  no request can go to an address you typed, and the bar takes no drops from other apps.
- **Remove deletes.** An item you remove is gone from your bar, whatever it was set to, and Undo brings it back as
  it was. Add items lists the types and nothing else: no remembered setups under a type, and no Forget.
- **A trash to drop on.** While you drag a row of your bar a strip comes up along the lower edge of the list, "Drop
  here to remove". It turns red under the row, and letting go there removes the item.
- **Remove an area.** With items in several areas each area's head ends in a trash: it removes the area and every
  item in it in one step, and says what went ("Removed Left and its 3 items") with Undo.
- **A new bar starts in Left.** A fresh install has its four items in Left, after the clock. A new item goes to the
  first area in use of Left, Middle, Right, and when the status bar is short of room Left has it first, then Right,
  then the Middle.
- **Notes with Undo stay three seconds.** "Removed…" in a row's place, the note for a closed or a removed area, the
  library's "Added" and Look's line go by themselves, or wait while the pointer or the keyboard is on them. With a
  screen reader's touch exploration on they stay until your next change. Ctrl+Z takes the change back after a note
  went too, until your next change or until you close the settings window.
- **Remove is a red button.** "Remove from the bar" in an item's options is red words and a trash on a pale red
  ground, beside Duplicate and as tall. Delete still removes the selected item. Look's Discard and Setup's Remove
  key are the same button.
- **Fix.** From right to left a dragged row hangs under the pointer.

### Upgrading from 2.1
- Your bar stays where it is, every item in the area it had, with everything it was set to.
- Shelf and Value from a URL items leave your bar, and what they kept on the device (a Shelf's texts and links, the
  addresses) is deleted at the first start. Nothing else of your layout changes.
- Items you had removed that waited under their type in Add items are gone; add the type again with +.
- A Prompter's notes and the flight a Flight item follows are now deleted with the item, once the removal can no
  longer be undone: at your next change to the bar, or when you close the settings window.
- With items in several areas, what the bar has only once stands in the first area in use from the left (it was the
  first from the right): Presenting's ‹, the empty capsule and "Add to"'s first choice. With items in Left too, the
  keyboard's "BentoBar menu" opens under Left's strip, and when room is short Left's items keep theirs before
  Right's do.
