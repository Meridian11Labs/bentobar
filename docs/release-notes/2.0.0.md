**BentoBar 2.0**: items in three areas of the status bar, a rebuilt settings window, and seventeen new items.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Optional online items.** Only **Weather** and **Air quality & UV** (Open-Meteo, no key), **Exchange rates** (Frankfurter, no key), **Crypto** (Kraken, no key), **Flight** (AirLabs, with a free key of your own), **US stocks** (Finnhub, with a free key of your own) and **Value from a URL** (an HTTPS address of your own) go online, and only after you set them up; **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/Meridian11Labs/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **Left, Middle and Right.** Items can sit in three areas of the status bar: a few on the left, some in the middle
  under the camera, the rest on the right. An area without room keeps its items behind the ‹ button. With everything
  in one area the bar looks as it did.
- **The settings window, rebuilt.** One Bar page: a preview of the bar, the list of what is in it, and beside it
  either the item library or the selected item's options. Drag a row to move it, into another area or out of the
  bar; or use a click or the keys. An item is in the bar or it isn't: one you set up and remove stays in the library,
  to bring back with + or to forget.
- **Seventeen new items:**
  - **Camera and mic**: shows while an app uses the camera or the microphone, and mutes the microphone for every app.
  - **Mirror**: you, from the camera, in a popup: a look before a call.
  - **Prompter**: your notes moving up under the camera, to read while you speak.
  - **Notification icons**: other apps' notifications as icons, one per app, and the list in its popup.
  - **Live activity**: the one thing happening now: a timer, a meeting, a flight, what plays.
  - **Shelf**: text and links you drop on it or copy, kept to copy or drag out later.
  - **Focus**: Do Not Disturb for a while, from a click; it ends by itself.
  - **Brightness** and **Keyboard light**: sliders in the bar.
  - **Headphones and output**: where sound plays.
  - **Connection**: says when you're offline, need to sign in, have no internet or use a VPN.
  - **Screen time**: how long the screen was on today, and the apps longest in front.
  - **Day progress**, **Sunset** and **Break reminder**.
  - **Crypto**: the coins you choose, from the Kraken exchange.
  - **Value from a URL**: one number or word from an address of your own, such as a sensor or a status page.
- **New access, each optional and asked for only by the item that needs it:** Camera (Mirror), Do Not Disturb
  access (Focus), Modify system settings (Brightness), and a second entry in Notification access, "BentoBar
  notification icons" (Notification icons). Nothing any of them reads is stored or sent.
- **Fixes.** Exchange rates loads again, from Frankfurter's current interface. Clipboard no longer pastes into other
  apps' fields (copy on click stays). A layout from a newer version of BentoBar is refused with a message instead of
  being cut down.

### Upgrading from 1.7
- Your bar keeps its look: every item starts in the area the bar was in.
- Items that were off with nothing set on them are gone; ones with a setup (a city, a flight, a rule) wait in the
  library under their type.
