**BentoBar 2.3.1**: fixes across the bar, Next meeting and the items, and less work while nothing changes.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Optional online items.** Only **Weather** and **Air quality & UV** (Open-Meteo, no key), **Exchange rates** (Frankfurter, no key), **Flight** (AirLabs, with a free key of your own) and **US stocks** (Finnhub, with a free key of your own) go online, and only after you set them up; **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/Meridian11Labs/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **Next meeting holds on.** When the calendar can't be read for a moment, Next meeting keeps what it had and tries
  again in two minutes; before, the meeting minutes away could vanish from the bar and the Live Update chip for a
  quarter of an hour. While a calendar syncs, its changes are read once they settle, in a few seconds, instead of
  once for every event it writes. Two meetings at the same time with the same title on one calendar (two interviews)
  are two, and so are two with different call links.
- **The bar.** A second finger on the strip no longer clicks an item or leaves one lifted mid-drag. Attaching or
  taking off a keyboard no longer closes an open menu. A menu card that ends in a divider opens.
- **The Bar page.** Holding Delete (or Backspace, or Ctrl+D) removes or copies one item, not one after another.
- **Items.** Battery and other percentages read as the system's (29%, not 28%). A wheel notch down over the Timer no
  longer starts a one-minute timer, and one at the end of Brightness leaves automatic brightness on. A Countdown on
  "Show when" shows its ✓ for a few seconds when the date comes. Exchange rates below 1 have four significant digits
  (0.00006123, not 0.0001). A flight whose times come without their time zone is followed until the service says it
  landed. A number that is no number in an Air quality reply is left out instead of stopping BentoBar. After a
  restart, a timer and keep awake follow the clock's changes again. Unmuting the microphone yourself while the bar
  is hidden means BentoBar leaves your microphone alone later.
- **Kept notes and flights.** A layout put aside at the start (one from a newer version, or one that can't be read)
  keeps its Prompter notes and followed flights until you put it back or discard it, and Undo never brings back an
  item whose notes it has already let go.
- **Less work.** The bar's colours are read only while the bar shows something; Screen time reads the day every ten
  minutes instead of every minute; Heat reads the CPU only while its menu is open; a change of a label or a slider no
  longer rebuilds the strip's look and the chip. A service that asks BentoBar to wait is left alone for an hour at
  most. The logs name what failed by its kind only, never a link or a place.
- **From 2.3.0**, for everyone coming from 2.2: Next meeting gets your meetings right (shared calendars, booked rooms,
  canceled and free events, a meeting during a long one), Setup's new **Calendar events** card says which events
  count, Next meeting can show the time alone or look past today, and the month view lists meetings. Its notes say
  more.
